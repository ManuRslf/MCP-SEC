Urban parks play an important role in modern cities. They provide residents with places to relax, exercise, and spend time with family and friends. Parks can also improve air quality by increasing the number of trees and plants in urban areas. In addition, they may help reduce heat during summer because vegetation provides shade and cools the surrounding environment. However, maintaining large public parks can be expensive, and cities must decide how much money to invest in them. Despite these costs, many researchers argue that green spaces contribute to both physical and mental well-being.
